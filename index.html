    Record two slow turns from the middle of a room. RoomScan works out depth from the video on your phone and builds a 3D model you can walk through and measure.

    Uses your phone's motion sensors for true-to-scale measurements. Walk slowly around the room.

    Camera won't open? Record with your phone's Camera app, then tap Use a video from Photos. If that picker doesn't open either, open this page in Safari or Chrome at claude.ai.

    How to record

    1. Stand near the middle. Hold the phone upright at chest height.
    2. Tilt down and turn a full circle. Keep the bottom of the walls and some floor in view. Take about 15 seconds.
    3. Tilt up and turn again. Get the top of the walls and the ceiling, and finish facing where you started. One video or two separate videos both work; pick both at once.

    Your scans

    Your video is processed on this device by an AI depth model (Depth Anything V2, Apache-2.0); the first scan downloads about 48 MB. Only if you allow it, 8 still frames are sent to Claude to find doors and counters for scale.

    The app sets real-world scale on its own from the floor, ceiling and phone height, and, if you allow it, from doors and counters Claude spots in the video. Floor area follows the room's real outline. Typical accuracy is about ±5%; the result shows its own estimate. For exact numbers, use Set a known length on one thing you've measured.
